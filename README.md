# Daftar — Simple Business Bookkeeping

**Daftar** is a lightweight business bookkeeping app designed to help small businesses and individuals keep track of customers, suppliers, transactions, and balances.

Built with small shops and independent business owners in mind, Daftar aims to make everyday record-keeping simpler, more organized, and accessible.

## Features

- **Customer management** — Keep customer records and track balances.
- **Supplier management** — Organize supplier information and transactions.
- **Transaction tracking** — Record payments, credit transactions, and account activity.
- **Product management** — Maintain a product catalogue for everyday business operations.
- **Barcode support** — Support for product-related barcode workflows.
- **Balance reminders** — Share balance reminders with customers through WhatsApp.
- **Data exports** — Export business records for external use and record-keeping.
- **Multiple languages** — English, Arabic, and French interfaces.
- **Local data storage** — Keep business records on your device.
- **Backup support** — Google Drive backup functionality, where configured.

### Installation

1. Download the APK onto your Android device.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If Android asks for permission to install apps from that source, allow it for your browser or file manager. Often times, you could extend the pop-up window and click on install anyway.
4. Follow the installation prompts.
5. Open Daftar and begin setting up your records.

Android may display a warning because the app is distributed outside Google Play. Only install APKs obtained from the official distribution links, as in the following.

## Data and Privacy

Daftar is designed for everyday business record-keeping. Business records may include customer information, transaction histories, and balances.

- Keep your device secure.
- Protect your backups and any exported files.
- Do not share business records publicly.
- Verify that backups are working before relying on them as your only copy of important data.

Review the app's actual backup and storage behavior before relying on any particular backup method.

## Updates

New versions may be published through GitHub Releases and Google Drive.

When updating, install a release signed with the same signing key as your existing installation. Keep a backup of important business records before updating.
